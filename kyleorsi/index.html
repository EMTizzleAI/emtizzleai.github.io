My account

Fifteen years of fighting to be heard.

My husband is Kyle Orsi. For fifteen years, I say I have been fighting him and his family through repeated legal conflicts. In my account, eleven other restraining orders were filed during that time.

I believe his family's wealth and access to trust-fund resources have created a profound imbalance. I have worked. I raised our daughter. I have tried to hold my life together while facing legal proceedings that I cannot afford to fight on equal terms.

From my perspective, the outcome has been devastating: I believe I have been pushed out of my daughter's life through the legal process. I want qualified legal counsel to review the actual court record and help me understand my rights and options.
